房俊杰Opening Government开幕政府支持

定义 · 立论 · 分工

Madam Speaker, we are not mourning a golden age; we are asking what a qualification now certifies. I will set a standard, then test it against one incentive.主席女士，我们不是在哀悼黄金时代，而是在追问学历今天究竟证明什么。我先立标准，再用一项激励去检验它。

PM-1Mode: Evaluative模式：EvaluativeDefinition / Standard规避 · 因果简化

First, fix what counts as good.第一，先确定什么算「好」。

Here is my evaluative argument claim:以下是我的评价型论点主张： Education now certificates recall more reliably than transfer.如今的教育，证明记忆复现比证明迁移能力更可靠。

Here is my evidence:以下是我的证据： World Bank data show世界银行数据显示， world adult literacy rose from 74.6% in 1990 to 87.6% in 2023 while the primary net enrolment rate moved from 82.0% to 89.2% — access improved on both measures全球成人识字率从 1990 年的 74.6% 升至 2023 年的 87.6%，小学净入学率从 82.0% 升至 89.2%——两项指标都显示入学机会改善

Here is my warrant:以下是我的论证依据： Those figures measure participation and basic skill, not the ability to apply knowledge in a new situation; a system can expand access and simultaneously narrow what its credentials certify. Definition of the object and Standard of judgement must therefore be fixed before any decline is asserted, and on the standard of durable transfer the burden falls on Government to show the incentive changed. This is a claim about an institutional signal, not about teachers' effort.这些数据测的是参与程度与基础技能，而非在新情境中运用知识的能力；一个体系完全可能同时扩大入学机会、又收窄其学历所证明的东西。因此在断言任何下降之前，必须先用「定义」确定对象、用「标准」确定评判尺度；以「可持久迁移」为标准，举证责任在于政府方证明激励确实变了。这是关于制度信号的主张，不是关于教师是否努力的主张。 In this evaluative claim, Definition fixes the object and Standard supplies the yardstick.在这条评价型主张中，「定义」确定对象，「标准」提供尺子。

PM-2Mode: Relational模式：RelationalComparison规避 · 后此谬误

Second, compare two assessment regimes.第二，比较两种评价制度。

Where high-stakes scoring dominates, coursework narrows toward rehearsal; where open problems are assessed, comparable cohorts show more transfer. The recurrence across systems makes the mechanism structural rather than a story about individual failure.凡高利害分数占主导，课程便收窄为反复操练；凡以开放性问题评价，可比群体表现出更多迁移。跨制度反复出现，说明这一机制是结构性的，而非个别教师失职的故事。

On that standard, the House should support.按此标准，本场应支持我方。

反对立场 · 驳 PM · 立论 · 分工

Madam Speaker, Government sets a new yardstick, then calls the old one a decline. Two answers.主席女士，政府方先立一把新尺子，再把旧尺子称为下降。两点回应。

LO-1Mode: Relational模式：RelationalDefinition / Alternativity规避 · 共因谬误

First, the curriculum changed shape.第一，课程的形状变了。

Here is my relational argument claim:以下是我的关系型论点主张： Expansion of aims is a better explanation of the same observations than decay.对于同样的观察，目标扩容比「衰败」是更好的解释。

Here is my evidence:以下是我的证据： UNESCO learning domains and World Bank series show联合国教科文组织的学习领域框架与世界银行序列表明， tertiary gross enrolment rose from 13.3% in 1990 to 43.2% in 2023, while syllabuses added digital, civic and collaborative competences高等教育毛入学率从 1990 年的 13.3% 升至 2023 年的 43.2%，课程纲要在读写之外加入了数字、公民与合作能力

Here is my warrant:以下是我的论证依据： Fixed hours spread over more goals redistribute attention rather than destroy it; cohort composition also shifts when participation nearly triples. Both are alternative causes of any apparent change, and neither requires learning to have fallen. Judging today's broader curriculum by yesterday's narrower syllabus mistakes a change of object for a change of quality.固定课时分摊到更多目标，是注意力的重新分配而非毁损；参与规模接近三倍时，群体构成也会改变。两者都是任何表观变化的替代原因，且都不要求学习真的下降。用过去更窄的纲要去评判今天更宽的课程，是把「对象变了」误当成「质量变了」。 Here, Definition and Alternativity supply competing causes.此处由「定义」与「替代原因」给出竞争性成因。

LO-2Mode: Descriptive模式：DescriptiveThoroughness规避 · 假类比

Second, ask who is in the average.第二，追问均值里都有谁。

An average over a near-universal cohort is not comparable with one over the selected minority in secondary school a generation ago; a description omitting that shift is not thorough.近乎全民化群体的均值，与上一代经过筛选才进入中学的少数人均值不可比；遗漏这一构成变化的描述并不周全。

PM-1 · 标准先行对象错位Intrinsicality

The fixed-standard argument treats recall as optional, yet recall is the substrate of transfer; defining good as transfer alone begs the question.固定标准的主张把记忆当成可有可无，但记忆是迁移的基础；只把「好」定义为迁移，本身已预设结论。

PM-2 · 制度复现共存非因果Correlativity

The cross-regime comparison lists coexistences, not a counterfactual with ability and cohort held fixed.跨制度比较列出的只是共存关系，而非在能力与群体均固定情况下的反事实。

Neither a fair comparison nor a measured fall has been offered. We oppose.公平比较与实测下降，两者都未被给出。我方反对。

驳 LO · 重建 PM · 新增论点 · 小结 OG

Madam Speaker, I will answer the composition objection, rebuild our standard, then add a mechanism you can observe in one classroom.主席女士，我将回应群体构成反驳、重建我方标准，再补上一个在一间教室里就能观察到的机制。

DPM-1Mode: Relational模式：RelationalReduction / Authority规避 · 因果简化

First, reduce the claim to one decision.第一，把主张缩减为一次选择。

Here is my relational argument claim:以下是我的关系型论点主张： Equal rewards flip one student from understanding to rehearsal.同等奖励会把一个学生从理解推向操练。

Here is my evidence:以下是我的证据： Behavioural research on incentives, cited as Authority here, finds此处以「权威」援引的激励行为研究发现， when a reproducible answer and an unseen problem earn identical credit, students shift effort toward the cheaper, more predictable task当可复现答案与陌生问题得到相同学分时，学生会转向更便宜、更可预期的任务

Here is my warrant:以下是我的论证依据： One student compares two costs for the same credit: understanding takes time and risks failure, rehearsal is cheap and predictable. Repeated across a term the cheaper choice becomes a habit and crowds out practice in transfer before any examination is sat. That micro-level mechanism produces the aggregate pattern PM described, and it survives the access objection, since a change in cohort composition does not change the choice facing any individual student.一个学生在相同学分下比较两种成本：理解耗时且有失败风险，操练便宜且可预期。一学期反复下来，更便宜的选择成为习惯，并在考试之前就挤掉了迁移练习。这一微观机制产生了首相所描述的总体图景，且不受「入学面扩大」反驳影响，因为群体构成的变化并不改变任何单个学生面对的选择。 In this relational claim, Reduction narrows the causal link and Authority supports it.在这条关系型主张中，「缩减」收窄因果链，「权威」为其提供支撑。

LO-1 · 目标扩容对象错位Absence

The broader-aims objection answers a claim about effort, not one about transfer: adding goals does not explain why equal credit still selects the cheaper route.目标扩容的反驳回应的是关于投入的主张，而非关于迁移的主张：增加目标并不能解释为何同等学分仍会选择更便宜的路径。

LO-2 · 测量人群构成不等于结果Measurement

The access objection shows averages moved, not that transfer within any group fell; composition cannot rescue the standard we set.入学面扩大的反驳只说明均值移动了，并未说明任一群体内部的迁移下降；群体构成救不了我方所设定的标准。

PM-1 · 标准先行声援 · DPMDefinition

I reaffirm the fixed standard: transfer is what makes a qualification worth having.我重申既定标准：迁移才是学历值得拥有之处。

The standard and the mechanism stand. We support.标准与机制均成立。我方支持。
